Highest-probability picks

The most likely outcomes the model finds today, one per match, from games with current stats. Outcomes above 90% are left out because bookmakers pay almost nothing on them. No bet is safe: a 75% pick still loses one time in four.

These are the model's fair odds (1 divided by the probability), not bookmaker prices. Outlined odds are the most likely outcome. GG and NG are both teams to score, yes and no. A dash means no prediction. Tap a match for the analysis and more markets.

Estimates come from a Poisson model with independent goals for each team. Corners use the same model, which tends to understate how much they vary. These are statistical estimates, not guarantees.
